Source: "Pixel Art Animations - Slashes" (itch.io asset pack).

slash_arc.png     128x128 pack, "Slash 3", color5  - crescent blade arc, 9 frames
slash_impact.png  128x128 pack, "Slash 1", color5  - multi-scratch burst, 9 frames

Both sheets are 640x256: a 5-column, 2-row grid of 128x128 cells, read in
row-major order. Nine frames are used; the tenth cell is empty.

color5 is the blue/cyan ramp (#00eeff -> #0059ff over a white core). It was
picked over the pack's other four (lime, red, purple, orange) because red is
reserved for blood and the cold ramp reads as steel against the dark levels.
The other colours are still in the original download if a second weapon ever
wants its own tint.
